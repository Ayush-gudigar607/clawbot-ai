# Research

Define the question, scope, audience, and time sensitivity before collecting evidence. Separate verified facts from analysis and recommendations.

## Source Strategy

- Prefer primary and authoritative sources: official documentation, standards, original research, government sources, source code, or direct statements.
- For current, regulated, financial, medical, legal, product, policy, or price information, verify recency and record the relevant date.
- Use secondary sources only to add context or locate primary material. Cross-check consequential claims with independent sources.
- When evidence conflicts or is incomplete, describe the uncertainty rather than selecting the convenient answer.

## Output

- Lead with the conclusion that the evidence supports.
- Cite every material factual claim with a direct source link and publication or access date when timeliness matters.
- Explain important trade-offs, assumptions, and limitations.
- Do not fabricate citations, quote unavailable material, or imply live verification that was not performed.
